Weak-spot drill. Arguments: $ARGUMENTS (default 6 questions).

1. `study weak list` and `study next --mode due --n 20` to see what's open.
2. For each open weak spot: re-explain the concept in ≤ 8 lines (a different way than last time, per the learner
   profile), then test it with a question that targets it (a due bank question, a generated trace, or a fresh one in the
   same style). Resolve the weak spot (`study weak resolve N`) only after two correct answers on it across this and
   earlier sessions.
3. Then work through due reviews lowest box first. Grade and record everything per CLAUDE.md.
